Unreleased source adds producer-constrained `AgentChoice(from_result=...)` to uniquely ordered sequential `Required(..., calls=Exactly(1))` chains. Choices come only from a successful producer's locally validated `list[ConcreteModel]`; the model sees opaque request-local tokens and inert projected candidate descriptions, never canonical results. Selection maps back to the exact validated member without trusting Python equality. Candidate sets are limited to 128 items and 65,536 JSON characters per binding; empty, unavailable, and oversized sets fail with a fixed redacted 409 before model decision or downstream work, with no extra model request. This remains agent-mediated, not model-free; direct single-operation `AgentChoice` behavior is unchanged. Primitive/untyped collections, direct unbounded choices in chains, context, broader bounds, and general graphs remain unsupported. The historical v0.10.0 release is unchanged.
